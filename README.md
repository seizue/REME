# REME

**Recipe & Business Management App**

REME is an offline-first recipe and business management app designed for small food businesses. It helps you track recipe costs, manage inventory, record sales, and monitor staff labor — all in one simple system, with no internet required.

## Features

### Recipe Costing
- Create recipes using ingredient-based breakdown
- Automatically compute total cost per batch, piece, or kilogram
- Apply customizable markup for selling price
- Include labor cost for more accurate pricing

---

### Sales Tracking
- Record daily sales sessions easily
- Track total revenue, expenses, and delivery fees
- Automatically compute net profit per session
- View summarized financial performance per day

---

### Inventory Management
- Maintain a complete ingredient database
- Track prices and updates over time
- Optional unit tracking for better accuracy
- Helps reduce overstock and wastage

---

### Staff & Labor
- Add staff profiles with salary details
- Assign pay periods (daily, weekly, monthly)
- Automatically factor labor cost into sales reports
- Better visibility of operational expenses

---

### Settings & Backup
- Backup and restore all app data
- Switch between light and dark themes

---

## Tech

- Flutter (Android)
- SQLite (offline, no login required)
- Provider for state management

---

## Why REME?

Built for small food businesses that need:
- Offline reliability
- Simple cost tracking
- Easy sales monitoring
- No subscription or internet dependency
- No ads
