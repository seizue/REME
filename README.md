# REME

**Recipe & Business Management App**

REME is an offline recipe and business management app for small food businesses. Track recipe costs, record sales, manage inventory, and monitor staff labor — all in one place, no internet needed.

## Features

- **Costing** — Build recipes with ingredients, apply markup on ingredient cost, add labor, and get the selling price per piece or per kg automatically
- **Sales** — Record daily sales sessions, track revenue, overhead expenses, delivery fees, and see net profit per session
- **Inventory** — Manage your ingredient library with optional unit tracking and price history
- **Staff** — Add staff members with salary and pay period, labor cost is prorated into your sales reports
- **Settings** — Backup and restore your data, switch between light and dark theme, report bugs

## Tech

- Flutter (Android)
- SQLite (offline, no login required)
- Provider for state management
